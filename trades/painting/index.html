What we do

Inside, outside, top to bottom.

We do the prep most painters skip: washing, scraping, patching and taping, so the finish lasts.

Exterior painting

Stucco, siding and trim, with power washing and crack repair before any paint goes on.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings and trim, with furniture covered and floors protected every day.

Cabinet painting

A new-looking kitchen for a fraction of new cabinets, with a hard, wipeable finish.

Decks & fences

Cleaning, sanding and staining that brings old wood back and protects it from the sun.

Trim & doors

Crisp lines on baseboards, doors and windows, the details that make a room look finished.

Prep & stucco repair

Patching cracks, fixing wood rot and priming, so problems don't come back through the paint.

How it works

Simple from the first call.

No pressure and no surprise charges. You'll know the colors, the schedule and the price before we start.

  1. Call or send photos

    Tell us which rooms or walls. A few phone photos help us give you a quick ballpark.

  2. Free estimate & colors

    We look at the surfaces, help you pick colors, and send a written, fixed price within two days.

  3. We paint and clean up

    Furniture and floors covered every day. We walk through it with you at the end.
