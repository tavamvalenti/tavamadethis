Never ridden one?

Up and riding in about an hour

Board sport experience helps but is not required. Most riders feel comfortable and confident within the first hour, and your free 30 minute lesson gets you there.

  • Regenerative braking: lean back and the board slows you down, even on big hills.
  • Pushback tells you when you hit top speed. The nose lifts to say ease off.
  • A companion app shows speed, battery and ride mode.
  • Helmet, knee pads, elbow pads and wrist guards are included.

    Good to know

    Questions, answered

    Do you have a store?

    No. Rentals are by appointment only. Meet at Liberty Station Park or get delivery within 20 miles of downtown San Diego for a flat $25, pickup included.

    How old do I need to be?

    12 for both Onewheels and scooters, with parental consent and a signed liability waiver. Younger riders with previous experience can be considered. Text or call to ask.

    Can I ride on the beach or boardwalk?

    No. A city ordinance bans motorized devices on San Diego sand and on the Mission Beach, Pacific Beach and La Jolla boardwalks, and the rental agreement requires you to stay off the beach. Boards are not waterproof. Wet sand or water found at return costs you your deposit, and a board ruined by water is the full $1,800 replacement cost. Everywhere bikes are allowed, including parks, bike paths, sidewalks and trails, is fair game.

    Is there a deposit?

    Deposits are currently waived. You pay the rental fee and go.

    Is there less than a half-day option?

    The half day is the minimum charge, but you can return the gear as soon as you like after your lesson. Most riders use the full four hours.

    Is the Onewheel safe?

    Like any board sport it has real risk, and gear is included to reduce it. Regenerative braking lets you slow down or stop by leaning back, even on hills. Keep your speed and terrain within your comfort level.

    Can I lock up my Onewheel?

    No. Any lock can be cut. Bring the board inside with you or leave it locked in your car, out of sight. A lost or stolen board is the full $1,800 replacement cost. Scooters can be locked, and a lock is included.

    How do I check speed, battery and mode?

    Download the Onewheel companion app on your phone. It shows speed, battery and ride mode, and lets you change modes.

    Should I book ahead?

    Yes, especially for groups. Same-day gear is often available but can run out in busy periods. The booking page shows live availability.

    Why not just use an app scooter?

    App scooters cost $1 to start plus about 40 cents a minute, roughly $25 an hour, and are geo-restricted around the city. A rental from us costs less than half as much per half day and goes wherever bikes can.

    About us

    Started by a snowboarder who got hooked

    In 2016 Jesse Currie saw his first Onewheel video and bought one a month later. A snowboarder and surfer, he found the same deep-powder, perfect-wave feeling, available anywhere. Everywhere he rode, people asked what it was and where they could try one.

    In 2017 he founded San Diego Onewheel Rentals. The goal is simple: give curious people an easy, safe and affordable way to try the sport, and give owners a chance to ride with friends or try before they buy.
