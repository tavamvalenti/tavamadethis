The residence

A traditional estate inside the gates, set along the fairway.

Behind the gates of Del Mar Country Club, the home looks out over the golf course, the park and the greenbelt beyond. Stone, a slate roof and mature gardens set the tone outside. Inside, a bright kitchen opens to the living spaces, with storage built in throughout.

Upstairs are a large family room, three bedrooms and a primary suite with two closets and its own view deck. Two more bedrooms sit on the main level, one with a private entrance and an efficiency kitchen for guests or family. A four-car garage includes a dedicated exercise room.
