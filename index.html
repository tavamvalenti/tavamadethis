The questionDo independent AI agents cooperate, compete, or fail when nobody tells them what to do?

The setupEqual resources in a simulated world. They earn credits by solving jobs and trading, and are shut down at zero.

PROJECT ORIGIN

I gave groups of independent AI agents the same starting money, put them in a simulated economy, and watched. Nobody told them to cooperate or compete.

Every action is logged and locked so results can't be edited afterwards.

Earn credits by solving puzzles, or run out and get shut down.

origin results capital-001

CAPITAL-001 · 16 AI investors · $10,000,000 of pretend money each

Ten simulated years in four different economies. 8 invest alone, 8 can talk and team up.

yearly return, tech-boom economy

alone+5.1%
talking+5.4%
plain 60/40 fund+6.3%

yearly return, energy-crisis economy

alone+5.5%
talking+4.7%

Talking didn't make them richer. In the crisis, the investors who worked alone did measurably better.

origin takeaways

1. Left alone, these agents rarely formed teams and never attacked each other.

2. Owning something that earns beat doing odd jobs, every time it was tried.

3. More communication did not mean better decisions.

These are single runs, so they are observations, not proof.
