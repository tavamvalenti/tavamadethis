Meet our staff

Local knowledge. Legendary waves.

SoCal Surf Lessons was founded in the summer of 2004 by lifelong waterman Rusty Friesen: a three-year California State Lifeguard, experienced big-wave surfer and bodyboarder, and world traveler.

What started as a small local operation is now a trusted, year-round surf school. Our instructors bring real-world experience from waves around the globe, and they’re patient teachers who know how to safely introduce beginners to the ocean while helping experienced surfers level up.

Our goal is simple: give you the knowledge, confidence, and stoke to enjoy surfing for life.

Questions & booking

Ready to paddle out?

What should I bring?

Bring a towel, sunscreen, water and come dressed in your bathing suit and ready to catch some waves.

What’s parking like? How early should I be?

Parking is street parking. On weekends and summer days it can be crowded, so plan to be there 10–15 minutes early to find a spot and walk over.

What should I expect once I’m there?

A typical beginner lesson includes about 5–10 minutes of beach instruction. The remaining time you’ll be in the water with your instructor surfing. You’ll also have free 1 hour use of the equipment after the lesson!

What will I learn?

Basic surfing fundamentals, as well as ocean awareness, water safety, wave riding skills, and surf etiquette.

Refunds for bad weather or bad surf?

Typically, we don’t take a deposit, and if your lesson is canceled due to weather there’s no charge. We do charge for late cancellations and no-shows, as our instructors are still paid for the time.
