Meet our staff

Built on a lifelong love of the ocean.

SoCal Surf Lessons was founded in the summer of 2004 by lifelong waterman Rusty Friesen. A three-year California State Lifeguard, experienced big-wave surfer and bodyboarder, and world traveler, Rusty built the company around his passion for the ocean and sharing the sport of surfing with others.

What started as a small local operation has grown into a trusted, year-round surf school. Our instructors bring years of real-world experience from waves across the globe, and more importantly, they’re patient teachers who know how to safely introduce beginners to the ocean while helping experienced surfers improve.

Whether it’s your first time on a board or you’re refining your technique, our goal is simple: give you the knowledge, confidence, and stoke to enjoy surfing for life.

FAQ

Good to know.

Still curious? Call or text us at 760-672-2770.

What should I bring with me?

Bring a towel, sunscreen, water and come dressed in your bathing suit and ready to catch some waves.

What is parking like, and how early should I be?

Parking is street parking. On weekends and summer days it can be crowded, so plan to be there 10–15 minutes early to find a spot and walk over.

What should I expect once I’m there?

A typical beginner lesson includes about 5–10 minutes of beach instruction. The remaining time you’ll be in the water with your instructor surfing. You’ll also have free 1 hour use of the equipment after the lesson!

What will I learn at the lesson?

Our students learn basic surfing fundamentals, as well as ocean awareness, water safety, wave riding skills, and surf etiquette.

Are there refunds for bad weather or bad surf?

Typically, we don’t take a deposit, and if your lesson is canceled due to weather there is no charge. We do charge for late cancellations and no-shows, as our instructors are still paid for the time.
